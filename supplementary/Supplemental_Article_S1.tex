\section{Contents of the supplemental data}

The supplemental material consists of this document and two archives. Supplemental Data S1 contains the code for data preparation, all models, the fusion baselines, the ablations, and the statistical analyses; the protocol written before the experiments, with its dated amendments; the logs of the learning-rate selection and of all seed runs; and the results for MovieLens-1M. Supplemental Data S2 contains the results for Amazon Reviews 2023 Video Games. Each results folder holds the learning-rate selection records, the per-seed result files with the per-user ranks of every model and variant under full-catalog and sampled evaluation, the per-user test ranks of the MF-BPR five-seed ensemble, and the aggregated results, per-user NDCG@10 values, and stratified analyses from which all tables and figures of the article are generated.

The rating data are not redistributed. The splits, sampled negatives, knowledge graphs, and deterministic baselines are regenerated from the public MovieLens-1M and Amazon Reviews 2023 files by the data-preparation step of the code; we verified that the regenerated data reproduce the aggregated results exactly. The same code and result files are available at \url{https://github.com/muniraja92/SYNAPSE-KG}. The README in Supplemental Data S1 and in the repository lists the files, the software versions, and the commands that regenerate the data and reproduce every reported number; the tables of this document are generated by \texttt{make\_supplement.py} from the stored result files.

\section{Additional results}

Table~\ref{tab:s_lr} reports the validation scores of the learning-rate selection. In 13 of the 14 selections the chosen learning rate lay at an edge of the grid of three values, in 11 cases at the largest value. Table~\ref{tab:s_det} reports the selected settings of the deterministic baselines; three of them lay at the upper edge of their grids. Both observations mean that wider grids could strengthen several models, including the baselines, as noted among the limitations of the article.

Table~\ref{tab:s_seeds} gives the full-catalog NDCG@10 of each seed for SYNAPSE-KG, MF-BPR, and the strongest single-model baseline, together with paired $t$-tests on the five seed means, and Table~\ref{tab:s_train} the checkpoint epochs, epochs run, and training times per seed. Tables~\ref{tab:s_full} and~\ref{tab:s_sampled} report all metrics under full-catalog and sampled evaluation for every single model and fusion baseline. Table~\ref{tab:s_gates} gives the ORACLE gate weights, the logistic-stacking coefficients, and the best single input selected in each seed. Tables~\ref{tab:s_strata_ml} and~\ref{tab:s_strata_vg} list the results of every stratum of the stratified analyses summarized in the article.
